Job Scout Drive Uploader

Job Scout Drive Uploader is a personal automation tool. Once a day it uploads job-search reports, generated by a private job-scout program, to its owner's own Google Drive.

It requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission, so it can see and manage only the files and folders it creates. It cannot read any other files in Google Drive.

It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.
