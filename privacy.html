Privacy Policy

Job Scout Drive Uploader · Last updated October 28, 2026

What the app does

Job Scout Drive Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner. It uploads job-search report files (CSV, Markdown and JSON) to a folder in the owner's own Google Drive.

Google data accessed

The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. With it, the app can create files and folders and manage only the files and folders it created. It cannot see, read or change any other content in Google Drive.

How data is used and stored

  • The app uses Google Drive only to create its report folder and to upload or update report files in it.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret and on the owner's own computer. It is never published or shared.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app does not collect data about anyone other than its owner.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files the app has already uploaded stay in Google Drive until they are deleted.
